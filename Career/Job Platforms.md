# Where to apply (and how to use each)

**1) Startup-focused (high signal, good for C/Linux + security)**

- **Wellfound (AngelList Talent)** – startup roles; salary/equity shown up front, direct to teams. ([Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) - Startup Job Search"))
    
- **YC Work at a Startup / YC Jobs** – single profile, founders can reach out; tons of SWE roles. ([Y Combinator's Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Software Engineer jobs at Y Combinator startups | Y ..."))
    
- **Hacker News “Who’s Hiring” (monthly)** – engineering-heavy companies post directly; you can browse via HN or the HN Hiring index. Apply within a few days of the post. ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45438503&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Ask HN: Who is hiring? (October 2025) - Hacker News"))
    

**2) Remote boards (use to discover, then apply on the company’s own site)**

- **We Work Remotely** – long-running remote tech board; filter for dev + security. ([We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "We Work Remotely: Advanced Remote Job Search"))
    

**3) AZ + regional tech hubs (your in-state advantage)**

- **Built In (Phoenix hub page)** – curated tech roles around Phoenix/Scottsdale/Tempe. ([Built In](https://builtin.com/jobs/phoenix-mesa-scottsdale?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Best Tech Jobs & Startup Jobs in Phoenix, AZ 2025"))
    
- **Arizona Technology Council job board** – local tech org listings. ([Arizona Technology Council](https://www.aztechcouncil.org/tech-jobs/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Arizona Job Boards - Tucson and Phoenix Tech Jobs"))
    

**4) Government / defense / cleared (security-leaning path)**

- **USAJOBS Cybersecurity & DHS Cybersecurity Service** – federal cyber roles; many list remote/hybrid options. ([cybersecurity.usajobs.gov](https://cybersecurity.usajobs.gov/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "gov.usajobs.cybersecurity"))
    
- **ClearanceJobs** – defense & intel contractors; filter for software/security (some require active clearance). ([ClearanceJobs](https://www.clearancejobs.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Security Clearance Jobs - ClearanceJobs"))
    

_(You can still use LinkedIn/Indeed/Glassdoor for breadth, but treat them as feeders—find a role there, then apply on the company’s **career site** if possible.)_

---

## How to work these sites efficiently

- **Prioritize company career pages (Lever/Greenhouse-hosted)** once you find a role; ATS parsing is cleaner and you avoid third-party friction.
    
- **Set alerts** on 3–5 of the sites above with keywords that match you:  
    `("C" OR "C/C++") AND (Linux OR POSIX OR "systems")`, `epoll`, `networking`, `embedded`, `security analyst`, `blue team`, `WAF`.
    
- **Weekly cadence:** 10–15 tailored apps (mix of remote, in-state, and 1–2 out-of-state hubs you’d relocate to).
    
- **Always check state eligibility** on “remote” jobs (some exclude AZ).
