
My name is Sam, I have known Jonah for most my life. we first met when we were children and had no perception as to where our lives would go. One minute we were sitting on a couch on wheels that had caught fire flying around our neighborhood roundabout and now here I am giving a speech at his wedding. Hopefully this speech goes better than how that couch ended up. I want to tell a story that starts when me and Jonah had just moved in together during college. We had just moved into to our new house and there was a knock at the door. Thats when we met three inconspicuous girls from across the street. We all introduced ourselves and gave them a tour of the house and that was that. A day or two passed and me and AJ were sitting on the couch watching tv and we heard this strange noise from the backyard. We both looked at each other and then went out to investigate. What could that have been? we thought. As we shifted closer to the sound... we saw two beady eyes stare back at us and there in the Dark was Jonah, with a pickaxe, digging holes in the dirt. You might be wondering why was he taking his first steps in college digging holes in our backyard and you would be right-- right where my head was at that night I saw him in the dark. I mean, What could possibly drive this man to toil away in the night to make himself look like he was losing his mind. The next night, I got home from work and walked into the house to hear chatter from the backyard. I took a step out back to find the yard illuminated by the light setup Jonah had put together. At first glance there wasn't much to it -- some string lights and wood beams held up by our hope that no strong winds would come. But after looking at the backyard a little more I thought was a good addition and eventually came to love being back there because of them. The midnight mining endeavor was still unexplained to me but, after taking another step I saw the true answer to why he went to such lengths. I didn't realize at the time, but Jonah had found something priceless, because right there, sitting next to Jonah was the girl from across the street, who we all know today as the bride, Sophia. From that moment on, I watched Jonah continue to show his persistence and affection, not by digging more holes in our backyard, but through his continuous efforts to prove to Sophia just how much he loved her. As I continued to get to know Sophia, I was so blessed to see her reciprocate that affection for him. I could tell that they had found something special and despite the fact that I was losing proximity and time with one of my best friends, I couldn't be happier with how much she made him smile and how much she made him laugh. As you begin this new chapter in your life, I want you to know Jonah, that accompanying you as your friend for all these years has been a gift and I am so proud of what you have accomplished and the man that you have become. Jonah is a cherished member of our friend group and I think I speak for all of them when I say we've never seen him happier than he is with Sophia. I pray that God blesses your marriage and you grow closer to each other through him. 

Sophia, we're so lucky to have you. 

And Jonah you found something truly special.

So let's raise a glass—to a lifetime of love, laughter, and happiness.

To Jonah and Sophia.
