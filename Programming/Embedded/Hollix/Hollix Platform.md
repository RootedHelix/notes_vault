## A Content Platform (Not Just a Device)

Right now the device is:

> A holographic display device.

That’s cool, but products that really succeed become:

> **A platform for content.**

This is what companies like **GoPro**, **Elgato**, and **Pico Interactive** did.

They didn’t just sell hardware — they sold **what you could _do_ with the hardware**.

# A Simple Version You Could Launch With

You don't need something complicated.

Version 1 could simply support:

- MP4 videos
    
- GIF animations
    
- PNG image sequences
    

Your firmware would automatically render them into **4 mirrored quadrants** for the pyramid.

Then users just drop files into a folder like:

```
/holograms/
```

and they appear in the device menu.
