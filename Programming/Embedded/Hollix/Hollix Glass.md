# Recommended Specs for Hollix

For your pyramid design, you would likely want:

```
Thickness:      1–2 mm
Reflection:     ~30–40%
Transmission:   ~60–70%
Material:       low-iron optical glass
Coating:        dielectric beam-splitter coating
```

Low-iron glass improves clarity and color accuracy. # Prototype Strategy I Recommend

For early development:

1. Buy **teleprompter beam splitter glass**
    
2. Cut it into **4 trapezoids**
    
3. Test pyramid geometry
    

This lets you validate:

- brightness
    
- distortion
    
- viewing angles
    

before ordering custom parts.

---

💡 **Important insight for Hollix**

Beam splitter glass will likely be the **single most expensive optical component** in the device, but it also has the **largest impact on perceived quality**.

Switching from normal glass → beam splitter glass can easily make the hologram look **3–4× brighter**.
