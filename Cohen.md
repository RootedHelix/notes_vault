# COHEN — THE CURSE OF BEING HIM

Create an absolutely ruthless, hilarious, melancholic, dark luxury-rap anthem about a man named **Cohen**, who runs a coffee shop and suffers from the unbearable burden of being the coolest man alive.

**Musical direction:** Atmospheric Toronto-style melodic hip-hop with haunting minor-key piano, deep distorted 808s, sparse trap drums, icy synthesizers, luxurious late-night production, dramatic silences, and explosive beat switches. The male vocalist has a deep, smooth, slightly raspy voice with effortless conversational rap delivery, emotional melodic hooks, sharp rhythmic punchlines, sarcastic spoken interludes, and an exhausted, supremely arrogant attitude. The performance should feel intimate, cinematic, emotionally detached, and devastatingly condescending.

**Central concept:** Cohen is genuinely depressed by how impossibly cool he is. He is a coffee-shop king surrounded by financially struggling customers, incompetent friends, mysterious nobodies, and people who cannot comprehend his greatness. He consistently wins at the casino, effortlessly cooks perfect San Diegans, and treats his own excellence as a terrible burden.

The comedy comes from Cohen delivering completely ridiculous, arrogant statements with the absolute seriousness of someone confessing his deepest emotional pain.

**Mandatory story elements:**

- Cohen runs the coffee shop like a private kingdom. He wears his apron like royal armor and views every breakfast shift as another day of carrying an ungrateful civilization.
    
- The shop serves a breakfast dish called a **San Diegan**. Cohen believes he makes the greatest San Diegan ever created and considers serving it to broke customers an act of charity.
    
- Cohen constantly refers to financially struggling customers as **"brokies."** Ruthlessly mock their declined cards, empty checking accounts, requests for discounts, and inability to afford breakfast.
    
- Cohen ALWAYS wins at the casino. Blackjack dealers fear him, slot machines surrender, roulette wheels obey him, and the casino considers him a financial threat. He doesn't gamble; he collects money.
    
- A completely irrelevant nobody named **Eddie** comes into the coffee shop and orders something called **"The Eddie."** Nobody knows who Eddie is, what he's accomplished, or why a menu item is named after him. Cohen becomes increasingly disturbed by Eddie's unexplained significance.
    
- Cohen's friend **Jonah** accidentally calls him **"Owen"** instead of Cohen. The music must stop completely as Cohen turns around and asks, in an ice-cold, bewildered voice: **"Jonah... who is Owen?"** Jonah desperately explains it was an accident, but Cohen repeatedly asks "Who is Owen?" as though Jonah has committed an unforgivable betrayal.
    
- Make a devastating joke about Jonah knowing hundreds of League of Legends champions but somehow forgetting Cohen's name.
    
- Include exaggerated backing vocals from customers, friends, dealers, and employees reacting to Cohen's impossibly arrogant statements.
    
- Cohen is never genuinely humble, never apologizes, and never admits that anything is his fault.
    
- The lyrics should be explicit, merciless, quotable, darkly funny, and packed with intricate internal rhymes, wordplay, sophisticated punchlines, and memorable callbacks.
    

**Song structure:**

[Intro — Cohen sighing over the espresso machine, lamenting his greatness]

[Verse 1 — Cohen describes the suffering of being impossibly cool while serving brokies]

[Hook — Emotional melodic chorus about the curse of always being better than everyone]

[Verse 2 — Cohen describes his effortless casino victories and the humiliation of returning to work after winning thousands]

[Verse 3 — The mysterious nobody Eddie ordering "The Eddie" and Cohen's existential confusion]

[Beat Switch — Jonah calls Cohen "Owen," followed by a devastating spoken confrontation]

[Verse 4 — Cohen continues lamenting his greatness, now questioning whether anyone truly deserves him]

[Final Hook — Powerful, layered vocals about loneliness at the top]

[Outro — Cohen closes the coffee shop, wins again at the casino, and wonders who Eddie is]

Write the COMPLETE song with all lyrics, dialogue, ad-libs, background reactions, and production directions.

The song should feel like a devastatingly serious, emotionally wounded rap confession about the most absurdly privileged problems imaginable.

**Most important:** Cohen should sound genuinely heartbroken that he is so cool, so wealthy, so talented at making San Diegans, and so incapable of losing at the casino. Never let him acknowledge the absurdity of his situation. His arrogance must be completely sincere.
